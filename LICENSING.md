# Licensing Strategy

OpenReactor uses a dual-track licensing strategy:

1. **Open Source License (default): AGPL-3.0-or-later**
2. **Commercial License (optional, future):** available from the project maintainer for organizations that need rights beyond AGPL.

## Why AGPL

OpenReactor is designed to be run as a network service. AGPL helps ensure improvements to the core are contributed back, including when software is modified and run over a network.

## Commercial Licensing Intent

We may offer a commercial license for organizations that need to:
- keep modifications private,
- embed OpenReactor in proprietary systems,
- avoid AGPL obligations,
- or obtain enterprise support/indemnity terms.

Commercial terms are separate from AGPL and require an explicit written agreement.

## Contributions and Relicensing

To preserve long-term sustainability and licensing flexibility, contributions are accepted under our contribution terms (see `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `CLA.md`).

Contributors retain copyright in their own work, but grant the project maintainer rights needed to distribute under AGPL and, where applicable, offer alternative commercial licensing.

## No Warranty

Unless stated otherwise in a separate agreement, OpenReactor is provided without warranty under AGPL terms.
