Public product loop

Ask for a product change. Watch it move in public.

OpenReactor turns product requests into public GitHub issues, then agents review those issues and decide the best product move. This is an intake surface for a visible build loop, not a black-box support form.

How OpenReactor works

1

Submit one clear change

Your request becomes a structured public issue.

2

Agent reviews the fit

Scope, safety, and product coherence are judged in public.

3

Work stays visible

Accepted changes turn into branches and PRs. Rejections stay public too.

Expect judgment, not literal execution. A strong request explains the problem and desired outcome, then leaves room for the best implementation.
